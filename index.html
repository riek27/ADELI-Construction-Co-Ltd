Our Purpose

Why CIDA Exists

CIDA believes that sustainable development begins with empowered communities. By working hand in hand with local people, government institutions, and international partners, we create practical solutions that address immediate humanitarian needs while laying the foundation for long-term peace, resilience, and inclusive development across South Sudan.

Who We Are

Dedicated to Community Transformation

CIDA is a national humanitarian and development organization established in 2015, working to improve the lives of vulnerable communities across South Sudan through protection, peacebuilding, and sustainable development.

Mission

Empower vulnerable communities through humanitarian and development initiatives.

Vision

A peaceful, resilient, and self-reliant South Sudan.

Values

Integrity, Accountability, Inclusion, Gender Equality, Sustainability.

Areas of Work

Our Programs

Protection

Strengthening protection systems for vulnerable populations, women, children, IDPs, and returnees.

Peacebuilding

Promoting peaceful coexistence through dialogue, mediation, reconciliation, and conflict prevention.

WASH

Improving access to clean water, sanitation facilities, hygiene promotion, and water infrastructure.

Food Security

Supporting vulnerable households through livelihood programs, agriculture, and resilience building.

Education

Increasing access to quality education, supporting schools, and promoting child protection.

Gender Equality

Advancing women's rights, preventing GBV, empowering women and girls, and inclusive participation.

Key Initiatives

Our Projects

Safe Water Access Initiative

Construction of community boreholes in Atab, Gulwei, Mayom Biong

Achievements: Increased access to safe drinking water, reduced walking distances for women and children, improved sanitation and hygiene, community water management training, sustainable borehole maintenance systems.

Partner: United Nations Mission in South Sudan (UNMISS)

Luony Akeer Police Post

Modern police facility in Gogrial East County with offices, armory, detention cells, and wash facilities.

Impact: Enhanced rule of law, improved public safety, reduced criminal activity, strengthened justice delivery.

Partner: UNMISS

Community-Based Protection Networks (CBPN)

Gogrial West, Tonj North, Tonj South

Objectives: Early warning systems, conflict monitoring, crisis reporting, peacebuilding, child protection, GBV prevention.
